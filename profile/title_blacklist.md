# Title Blacklist (Hard Initial Filter)

Positions whose job title contains any of the following terms will be immediately discarded before categorization, parsing, or ranking.

This blacklist should only contain terms that indicate a clearly incompatible seniority level, professional area, or type of position. Ambiguous titles should proceed to categorization instead of being discarded here.
